# Demonstration shop policy

This is fictional training material. It is not a policy for any real business.

## Refund period
Customers can request a refund within 30 days of delivery. The product must be unused and in its original packaging. A proof of purchase is required.

## Delivery
Standard UK delivery takes 3 to 5 working days. Express delivery takes 1 to 2 working days. Orders over GBP 75 qualify for free standard UK delivery.

## Support
Support is available Monday to Friday from 09:00 to 17:00 UK time. Customers should contact the support team before returning an item.
